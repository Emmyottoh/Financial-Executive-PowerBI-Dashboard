# Financial Executive BI & Portfolio Analytics Suite

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive, two-page Microsoft Power BI financial analytics dashboard designed to examine sales performance, profitability, geographical revenue distribution, product performance, and historical sales trends.

<img width="1671" height="941" alt="Interactive Financial Suite Dashboard-BI" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16dda3df-35d1-49a7-8d78-70bf543f0035" />

## Executive Performance Highlights

- **Total Sales:** $118.73M
- **Total Profit:** $16.89M
- **Profit Margin:** 14.23%
- **Geographical Analysis:** Five markets — United States, Canada, France, Germany, and Mexico
- **Historical Analysis:** Sales trends covering 2013–2014

## Technical Stack & Tools

- **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Data Preparation:** Data cleaning, transformation, and organization
- **Analytical Techniques:** Financial KPI analysis, descriptive analytics, geographical comparisons, and product performance analysis
- **Core Competencies:** Data visualization, financial intelligence, executive reporting, and business performance analysis

## Key Features & Business Impact

- **Executive Financial Overview:** Presents consolidated sales, profit, and profitability indicators.
- **Geographical Revenue Analysis:** Compares financial performance across five international markets.
- **Product Intelligence:** Examines product-level sales contributions and performance rankings.
- **Historical Trend Analysis:** Visualizes sales patterns across 2013–2014.
- **Executive Decision Support:** Organizes financial indicators into accessible visual reports for performance evaluation.

## Data Pipeline & Methodology

1. **Data Preparation:** Organized and prepared financial records for analytical reporting.
2. **Financial KPI Analysis:** Evaluated sales, profit, and profit-margin indicators.
3. **Dashboard Development:** Built two Power BI reporting pages presenting financial performance and sales trends.
4. **Executive Reporting:** Structured dashboard visuals to communicate financial insights and support business decisions.
